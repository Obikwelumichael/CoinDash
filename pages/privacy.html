Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Important: This notice describes CoinDash as currently implemented. Before a production launch, the operator should confirm hosting, backup, provider, retention, and international-transfer arrangements and obtain legal advice appropriate to the places where CoinDash is offered.

This Privacy Policy explains how CoinDash handles information when you browse its cryptocurrency market dashboard, create an account, save a watchlist, configure alerts, or use account settings. Cookie consent and acceptance of the Terms of Use are separate decisions; choosing cookie preferences does not accept the Terms.

Applicable privacy law

CoinDash operates from Nigeria, where the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 governs applicable processing of personal data. Other privacy laws may also apply to our activities involving users in other countries. The service's informational purpose does not remove applicable privacy obligations or your statutory rights.

Information handled by the application

Account and security information

If you register, the application stores your display name, normalized email address, an Argon2id password hash, account timestamps, and email-verification status. It does not store your password in plaintext. If you enable two-factor authentication, an encrypted TOTP secret and hashed recovery-code records are stored. Password-reset and email-verification records contain token hashes and expiry/use status, not the raw tokens.

Account features

The database stores your saved CoinGecko coin IDs, supported account preferences (currently theme and whether alert emails are requested), market-alert conditions, and in-app alert notifications. Alert records may include the coin, threshold, direction, observed value, read status, and timestamps. These records are associated with your account.

Technical and operational information

Account sessions are stored server-side in PostgreSQL and referenced by an opaque session cookie. Rate-limiting middleware processes a client network address in memory to restrict repeated authentication and account actions. The application does not currently write IP addresses or browser-agent strings to its user database and does not run a dedicated web access-log middleware. On a successful sign-in, CoinDash prepares a security-notification email containing the sign-in time, IP address, and a simplified device/browser description; those details are not saved in the CoinDash user database. Application errors may be written to server logs. Hosting, database, email, and network providers may maintain their own technical logs; their exact fields and retention periods must be confirmed with those providers.

Cookies and browser storage

Necessary session cookie: signed-in functionality uses the HTTP-only cryptodash.sid cookie. It is marked Secure in production, uses SameSite=Lax, and has a rolling maximum age of seven days. It supports authentication and security and cannot be disabled through the optional preferences panel without making sign-in unavailable.

Cookie choice record: the consent interface saves a versioned record containing your decision, category flags, and timestamp in this browser’s localStorage. This record is not a cookie, is not sent to the server, and does not contain account credentials.

Session storage: the Trending page uses tab-scoped sessionStorage for a small previous-rank snapshot used to show rank movement. It is not used to authenticate you and is cleared by the browser when the tab session ends.

The current application has no optional analytics, advertising, or marketing tracker integration. The account theme and email-alert preferences are stored in your account database, not in optional browser cookies. If optional storage or tracking is added later, this page and the consent version/categories must be reviewed before activation.

Why information is used

Account data is used to create and secure your account, authenticate requests, deliver requested verification/reset messages, and provide the watchlist, preferences, alerts, and notifications you use. Technical processing supports session operation, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and service reliability. The consent record remembers the choice made in this browser. Market and news requests provide the dashboard features you request.

Service providers and external links

CoinDash uses Reonr (reonr.com) for application hosting in Nigeria, as confirmed by the operator. The PostgreSQL database used for account data is also hosted in Nigeria.

  • CoinGecko supplies market prices, search/trending data, market statistics, and chart history through server-side API requests.
  • Binance supplies exchange candlesticks for supported intraday chart intervals through server-side public market-data requests.
  • Cryptocurrency.cv supplies the aggregated crypto-news feed. Article links may take you to the original publisher, which has its own privacy practices.
  • Brevo is the application's email integration. When email delivery is configured, it processes recipient email addresses and message content for verification, password resets, sign-in notifications, welcome messages, and requested market alerts.
  • Telegram is used only when you choose to link a Telegram account and enable Telegram alerts. CoinDash stores the linked Telegram chat ID, user ID, and, when available, username. Telegram receives the alert message and destination chat ID needed to deliver that alert.
  • Google Fonts and the unpkg CDN are contacted by the browser to load fonts and the TradingView Lightweight Charts library. Those providers receive ordinary network request information such as your IP address and request metadata.

Market/news APIs are called by the CoinDash server, so those providers ordinarily receive the server’s network address and request details rather than your account session cookie. The browser directly contacts font/chart asset hosts. The app does not intentionally send your password or account session cookie to these market-data or asset providers. Confirm current provider terms, data locations, and any contractual requirements before launch.

Retention and deletion

Account records and related watchlist, preference, alert, notification, and security records remain in the database while the account exists. The account-deletion flow deletes the user and related rows and invalidates stored sessions. Session rows expire according to their expiry timestamp; password-reset links expire after 30 minutes and verification links after 24 hours. The code marks token rows used, and related one-time tokens cannot be used again after expiry or use.

Account deletion does not necessarily remove data immediately from provider systems or backups. Retention for application logs, infrastructure records, provider records, and backups depends on the applicable provider and backup-rotation configuration. The operator will update this notice when those periods are defined.

Security

The application uses Argon2id password hashing, server-side sessions, HTTP-only session cookies, encrypted TOTP secrets, hashed recovery codes, expiring single-use email tokens, and request rate limits. Production session cookies are configured Secure. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security; protect your email account and use a unique password.

You can change or withdraw optional browser-storage preferences at any time using Cookie Settings. No optional analytics or marketing categories are currently active. You can delete your account from Account Settings; account deletion removes the database records listed above, subject to backups, provider records, or any retention required by applicable law.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request information, access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or to object to certain processing or complain to a regulator. To make a privacy request, contact Saintdevopz@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify your identity and aim to respond within 30 days, subject to applicable law and any permitted extension.
